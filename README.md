# www: vanity veils violence

A browser in three chapters by **Lucia Rebolino**, made during the 22nd Web Residency
»[Ignore All Previous Instructions](https://www.akademie-solitude.de/de/postproject/web-residencies/calls-2026/ignore-all-previous-instructions/)«
at [Akademie Schloss Solitude](https://www.akademie-solitude.de/de/www-vanity-veils-violence/), 2026.

`www` only browses `.ai`, the country code of Anguilla, an island in the Caribbean that earns a large part of its
income from selling its two letters to the AI industry.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** — for Mac (macOS 12 or later):

- **Apple silicon** (M1 and later): `www-mac-arm64.zip`
- **Intel**: `www-mac-intel.zip`

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" is Apple silicon, "Processor: Intel" is Intel.

## Opening it the first time

`www` is not distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm it once:

1. Unzip the download and drag **www** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app: click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about www, click **Open Anyway**,
   and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

From then on it opens like any other app.
